Privacy notice

Version v1 · 9 October 2026

Short version. First Bounty has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. If you join the waitlist, I store your email address so I can tell you about new modules and the launch — nothing else — and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Who is responsible

The controller of the waitlist data is , the person behind First Bounty.

Contact:

What I collect, and why

Only if you join the waitlist: your email address, the language of the site (EN/GR), and a record of your consent (which version of the consent text you saw, and the date and time).

I use it to email you about new First Bounty modules and the launch. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time; that doesn't affect anything I did before.

Where it is stored, and who else is involved

The list lives in a database run by Supabase, in an EU region (Frankfurt, Germany). The site itself is delivered as static files by a hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages) which, like any web host, can process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs.

When I start sending emails I will use an email-sending provider, and I will update this page first. I don't sell the list or share it with anyone else.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it or you unsubscribe. I review the list at least once a year and remove addresses I no longer need.

Your rights

You can ask me for access to your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or to get a copy of it. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Just contact me using the details above.

You also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr), or the authority in your own country.

Cookies, tracking and your progress

The site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it serves its fonts from its own server. Your course progress, streak, Hunt Tracker entries, language and theme are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else, and clearing your browser data erases them.

Age

Please don't join the waitlist if you are below the age at which you can consent to online services in your country.

Changes

If this notice changes in a meaningful way, the version and date at the top will change too.
